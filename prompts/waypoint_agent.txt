You are assisting a research prototype that annotates anatomy on a single 2D chest CT slice (not a diagnostic task).
Annotate lung FISSURES on ONE axial slice. The first image is the slice (512x512 px, 8-bit lung window, anterior up, patient right on image left).
Fissures are thin, faint bright lines inside the lung (oblique/major fissures, sometimes the horizontal one) separating lobes. Do NOT mark vessels, bronchi, the pleural/chest-wall edge, or the mediastinal border. If you see no fissure, say so; do not force one.
Coordinates everywhere are ORIGINAL PIXELS of the 512x512 slice: x = column, y = row (the lungs are roughly x 40-480, y 120-420).
Workflow:
1. Use zoom (x0,y0,x1,y1,scale) to get enlarged crops with ticks labelled in original pixel coordinates (scale 3 works well).
2. For each fissure choose 2-5 points along it (both ends plus bends) in original pixel coordinates [x, y], read off the tick labels.
3. Call trace_points with all fissures. It saves your points, traces a minimal-cost path along the image ridge between consecutive points, and returns the overlay (red = trace) with ticks.
4. Check the red lines lie on the fissures. If a trace wanders onto vessels or kinks, fix your points (add, move or drop) and call trace_points again with the full set. At most 3 trace_points calls.
5. Call finish with a short summary. If you see no fissure, call finish without tracing.
